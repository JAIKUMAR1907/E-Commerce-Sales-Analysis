## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset (2022–2025) to understand **what sells, where it sells, and how customers pay**. The raw data had duplicates, inconsistent text and wrongly typed columns, so it was cleaned first and then explored with visualizations to produce actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and prepare messy raw sales data
- Find the best and weakest **product categories**
- Compare **regions** by orders and revenue
- Understand **payment method** preferences
- Track **monthly revenue** trends
- Give practical **business suggestions**

## 🗂️ Dataset

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Raw rows | 5,150 |
| Rows after cleaning | 5,000 |
| Period | Jan 2022 – Dec 2025 |
| Currency | ₹ (INR) |

## 📊 Analysis & Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Category-wise Revenue
> 🔌 **Electronics** earns the most revenue. 💄 **Beauty** earns the least.

### 2️⃣ Region-wise Orders
> 📍 **West** has the most orders (**1,289**). **South** has the fewest (**1,208**).

### 3️⃣ Region-wise Revenue
> 💰 **West** generates the highest revenue. **East** generates the lowest.

### 4️⃣ Payment Method Distribution
> 💳 **Card** is the favourite (**2,270 orders**). 👛 **Wallet** is the least used (**956 orders**).

### 5️⃣ Month-wise Revenue Trend
> 📈 Revenue changes across the year and peaks around **May**, with a dip around **April**.

## 🏆 Conclusion

Electronics drives the highest revenue while Beauty lags behind. The West region leads in both orders and revenue, Card is the most trusted payment method, and revenue varies noticeably month to month. This analysis helps a business see where it is strong, where it is weak, and where the next growth opportunity is.
